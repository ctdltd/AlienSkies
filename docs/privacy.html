Alien Skies privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Certification Tool and Die, Ltd.

Alien Skies is a screen saver and live wallpaper that shows the night sky as seen from planets around other stars. This policy covers the Alien Skies app for Android and the Alien Skies screen saver for Windows.

The short version

Alien Skies does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your settings, such as the chosen planet, viewing direction and any imaginary planets you create, are saved only on your device: in the app's private storage on Android, and in %APPDATA%\AlienSkies on Windows. They never leave the device through Alien Skies. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include these settings in your personal backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, and we cannot read it.

Permissions

On Android, Alien Skies does not request the internet permission or any permission that gives access to your personal data, location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. The system binds it as a screen saver or live wallpaper only when you choose it.

Star and planet data

The star and exoplanet catalogs are built into the app. They come from the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA) and the NASA Exoplanet Archive, and are read locally.

Children

Alien Skies collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
